Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Welcome to ChartMyFreedom.com. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle information on our website and clarifies our strict commitment to data minimization.

1. No Personal Data Collection

ChartMyFreedom.com does not collect, store, or process any personal data. We do not require account registration, we do not use contact or newsletter forms that capture names or email addresses, and we do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) from our visitors.

2. Non-Specific Location and IP Tracking

We do not track individual or specific user locations. The only location-related data monitored is general user location (at a broad country, state, or city level) based strictly on where a visitor's IP address is generally registered. This data is used solely in aggregate forms to analyze overall regional traffic patterns and cannot be used to pinpoint your precise physical address or identity.

3. Approved Third-Party Tracking Tools

To optimize our content and safely serve advertisements, ChartMyFreedom.com utilizes a specific, restricted suite of official Google products. These are the only tracking tools active on this site:

  • Google Tag Manager (GTM): Used exclusively to safely deploy and manage scripts on our website. GTM itself does not store or collect personal data.
  • Google Analytics (GA4): Used to measure anonymous, aggregated website traffic and visitor interactions (such as page views and session durations). GA4 is configured to respect data minimization guidelines, using anonymized IP data to provide generalized, non-specific geographic insights.
  • Google AdSense: Used to display advertisements. Google uses cookies (including vendor advertising cookies) to serve relevant ads based on a user's previous visits to this site or other websites.
  • Google Search Console: Used strictly for search optimization and performance monitoring. It tracks aggregate search queries that bring users to our platform and does not monitor individual identities.

4. Cookies and Advertising Preferences

Google and its partner vendors use cookies to serve ads on ChartMyFreedom.com. You can manage your tracking preferences or completely opt-out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting the official Google Ads Settings page.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, analytics and advertising cookies are only set after you allow them in our cookie banner. Visitors anywhere can change or withdraw their choice at any time in Cookie settings.

5. Compliance and Frameworks (GDPR & CCPA)

Because ChartMyFreedom.com avoids collecting personal data or precise geographic tracking, our core infrastructure inherently adheres to stringent regional regulations including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

6. Contact Channels

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our tracking configurations, please inspect the public infrastructure details provided on our main domain layout.
