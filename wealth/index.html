Freedom Ledger

Your road to financial independence

Nobody gets rich from an app. People get rich by keeping a wide gap between what they earn and what they spend, clearing expensive debt, and letting invested money compound for years. This ledger shows exactly how far away that is for you, and which lever moves it most.

No information is collected. Everything you type stays on your own device. There are no accounts, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing you enter is sent to any server. (The page only downloads its fonts from Google Fonts; none of your numbers go with that request.)

Your numbers are saved only in this browser so they're here next time; "Reset to example" clears them. Emailing your results opens your own email app with a draft addressed to you. We never see it.

    Disclaimer

    Freedom Ledger is an educational calculator, not financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and it is not a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Projections assume a steady return every year; real markets rise and fall, and past performance does not guarantee future results. Results depend entirely on the numbers you enter and may be wrong for your situation. Talk to a qualified, fee-only financial professional before making decisions.

    How the model works: it runs month by month in inflation-adjusted dollars. Each month's surplus fills your emergency fund first, then pays debts whose APR is above your expected return (highest first), then goes into investments.

    Privacy: no information is collected, stored on a server, or shared. Data you enter is kept only in your browser's local storage on this device.
