What to do next

The standard order of operations. Each step is checked against your numbers.

    Debt payoff order

    Pay minimums on everything, then put every spare dollar toward the highest rate that beats your expected return. Cheaper debt just gets its minimum.

    How it works

    Nobody gets rich from an app. People get there by keeping a wide gap between what they earn and what they spend, clearing expensive debt, and letting invested money compound for years. The model runs month by month in today's dollars: spare money fills your emergency fund, then pays debt above your expected return, then gets invested.
