    Common questions

    How does this retirement calculator work?

    It projects your finances month by month in today's dollars. Each month, the money left after spending first builds your emergency fund, then pays off debt with an interest rate above your expected investment return, then gets invested. You can retire when your investments reach your freedom number.

    What is a freedom number?

    It's the amount you need invested to cover your yearly spending from your investments alone. It equals your yearly spending divided by your withdrawal rate. At a 4% withdrawal rate, that is 25 times your yearly spending.

    What withdrawal rate should I use?

    Many people start with 4%, based on historical studies of how long a stock and bond portfolio lasted over 30-year retirements. A lower rate, such as 3.5%, is more conservative and suits longer retirements.

    Is my financial information stored or shared?

    No. Everything you type stays in your browser on this device. Nothing you enter is sent to a server, and emailing your results opens a draft in your own email app.
